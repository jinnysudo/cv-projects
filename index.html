Dhyanam's Python Projects

Computer vision, voice, NLP and desktop apps, all running live in your browser. Camera and mic never leave your device.

Face Detection

Finds the first face in your webcam feed and draws a box around it, like face_detection.py (OpenCV Haar cascade). In the browser it uses Google's BlazeFace model.

Camera is off.

Hand Gesture Recognition

Tracks up to 2 hands (21 landmarks each), counts raised fingers and names the gesture, same logic as hand_gesture.py.

  • Victory index + middle up
  • Open Palm all five up
  • Fist all fingers down

Camera is off.
