Dhyanam's CV Projects

Python computer-vision & voice projects, ported to run live in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: camera and mic stay on your device.

Face Detection

Finds the first face in your webcam feed and draws a box around it, just like face_detection.py (OpenCV Haar cascade). In the browser it uses Google's BlazeFace model.

Camera is off.

Hand Gesture Recognition

Tracks up to 2 hands (21 landmarks each), counts raised fingers and names the gesture, same logic as hand_gesture.py.

  • Victory index + middle up
  • Open Palm all five up
  • Fist all fingers down

Camera is off.

Mini Alexa

A voice assistant from mini_alexa.py. Try: "what's your name", "who created you", "what time is it", "open google", "who is Albert Einstein", "tell me about Python", "bye".

Voice works best in Chrome or Edge.

Stopwatch

The Tkinter stopwatch app (stopwatch.exe), rebuilt for the web with the same layout: HH:MM:SS with Start, Stop and Reset.
